Overview
EurekaDev is a 5-week online innovation competition open to high school students worldwide that combines the spirit of a science fair with the creativity of a hackathon. Participants can code or research projects in diverse categories and present their work in a final symposium. Along the way, students will attend weekly workshops, hear from industry professionals, and gain valuable skills in problem-solving, teamwork, and pitching.

Whether you’re an experienced builder or a complete beginner, we encourage you to join! You can compete individually or with up to 4 teammates. Throughout the competition, there will be workshops and guest speaker panels covering computer science, AI, medicine, and more, so everyone has the chance to learn, innovate, and pitch their ideas.

 

What makes EurekaDEV unique?
Tracks

We offer two tracks so participants can showcase their strengths:

Coding Track → apps, AI models, technical solutions
Research Track → literature reviews, experimental designs, theoretical innovations
Subject Categories

Within each track, participants can choose from 6 categories:

Computer Science + AI (Technology)
Biology/Medical and Environmental Science
Math & Physics
Economics
Problem in the Community

Why join?
EurekaDev isn’t just about competing, it’s about learning, collaboration, and creating impact. Whether you want to code, research, or both, this is your chance to innovate across multiple categories and share your vision with a global community. Additionally, there's a chance to win awesome prizes and scholarships to research programs!

Requirements
What to Build
You must create a project in one of two tracks and in one of the subject categories:

Coding Track: Build a functional prototype, app, website, simulation, or other coded solution. Citations and evidence in your project description is a bonus!

Research Track: Conduct background research and create a literature review, theoretical framework, or experimental design around your chosen problem. A prototype is a bonus!

What to Submit
Project Video (Required) (Paste link in Video demo link field)

Maximum 4 minutes in length.
Must explain the problem, your solution, and how it works.
Can include demo footage, slides, or other visuals.
